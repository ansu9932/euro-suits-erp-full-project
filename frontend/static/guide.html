Sign in to your workspace

  1. Choose the dashboard assigned to you below.
  2. Enter the user ID and password provided privately by your administrator or in your welcome email.
  3. Complete email verification if enabled for your account.

Each link opens the live ERP. Your company, responsibilities and available features are controlled by your administrator. A dashboard link does not grant extra access.

Administrator: welcome a new user

  1. Open User access and choose Add user.
  2. Enter the employee’s name, email address, mobile number with country code, user ID and initial password.
  3. Select their designation, company, reporting manager and permitted responsibilities. Assign teams, lines or orders where required.
  4. Save once. The confirmation reports whether the welcome email was accepted for delivery.

The welcome email contains the assigned role, responsibilities and private sign-in details. If delivery fails, check the email issue report before taking further action; the account has already been created.

Create and assign a team

In User access, open Teams and add a team for the appropriate company. Then edit the employee’s access, choose the team-based scope and assign the relevant teams. Save the changes. Only an authorized administrator can change these assignments.

Need help?

Contact your administrator for missing features, account changes or sign-in assistance. Use your assigned account and keep its sign-in details private.
