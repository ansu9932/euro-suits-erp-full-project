Fabric, trim and development masters

From an operational dashboard, select Materials & samples in the left menu. The library is shared within each assigned internal company.

  1. Finance → Company Setup: register a Supplier, enter its company details, supplier code, dial code, fax and contact people. Contacts include phone/mobile, email, website, Skype and speciality. Save and submit; MD or Super Admin approves. Only approved registered suppliers appear in the new sample form.
  2. Fabric Sourcing Manager → Master lists: add Category, then its Weave, then Sub-item. Add Composition, Colour, Product, Season, Swatch type and Finish type as needed. Edit or archive values; archived choices stay in historical records.
  3. Fabric library → Add fabric sample: choose the internal company, approved supplier and reference origin. India generates ESF; Dubai generates ETF. Complete Specifications, Measurements, Commercial, Sample details and Additional. Category and weave must match. Keep old references in the separate legacy-reference field.
  4. Measurements: add quoted weight/width rows and optional ranges/actual values. The calculator converts inches/metres to centimetres and GSM/ounce weight to grams per linear metre. A positive width is needed for linear-metre weight conversion.
  5. Save, then View: upload multiple PNG/JPEG photos. Maximum 12 per fabric sample, 5 MB each and 16 megapixels. Photos are available only to users with commercial material access. Open Barcode label, choose copies and print. The CODE128 barcode contains only the permanent reference.
  6. Change a price: Edit sample → Commercial → Save revision. Price & change history retains prior price, currency/unit, MOQ, quote date, author and timestamp. Reopening is required if another user has saved first.
  7. Search / scan and export: search by reference or specifications, filter category/weave/composition/colour (commercial users also get supplier and received dates), then Export Excel. Exports enforce the same visibility as the API.
  8. Trim library: Trims Sourcing Manager or Super Admin registers trim references, dimensions, individual unit, units per pack and pack unit. Photo upload belongs to fabric samples. Merchant trim-entry is not enabled while its supplier/commercial privacy rules remain unresolved.
  9. Merchant comparison: use test.junior.merchant. Fabric views and exports contain reference, category, weave, composition, weight, width and colour. Supplier identity, article, price, actual inspection values, photos and revision history stay hidden.
  10. Supplier ratings: MD or Super Admin opens Materials & samples → Supplier ratings → Update rating. Blocked/Delisted needs a reason. It prevents new or edited samples, photo uploads and new development selections/approvals using that supplier. Restore the practice supplier to Good after testing. Existing records remain visible. Legacy purchasing uses its existing separate supplier records and is not yet connected to these restrictions.
  11. Development masters: merchant/development users create a style, select their assigned buyer, record season/fit/waist, charge type and amount, request email reference and linked fabrics/trims. Save draft or submit. MD/Super Admin can approve or request changes. Approved/submitted records are locked. The request-email field stores a reference, not an uploaded email or an outgoing message.
