EURO SUITS MFG · ERP

Privacy policy

Effective date: 2 October 2026

This policy explains how Euro Suits MFG uses information in its internal enterprise resource planning service at erp.eurosuitsmfg.in, including work notifications sent through email and WhatsApp. It applies to authorised ERP users and people whose business information is entered into the ERP.

Privacy contact: it@eurosuitsmfg.com. You do not need an ERP account to contact us about your data.

1. Information used by the ERP

  • Account and work-contact details, such as name, username, email address, telephone number, role, assigned company, team and access permissions.
  • Operational records entered by authorised users, such as orders, sample requests, materials, supplier and buyer details, company-registration documents, approvals, assignments and uploaded files.
  • Security and service records, including sign-in activity, IP addresses, account changes, errors and audit records. Passwords are stored as hashes, rather than as readable account passwords.
  • Notification preferences, consent records, message content and delivery information, including recipient number, message identifiers, status and errors.

2. Why information is used

Information is used to provide ERP access, apply role and company permissions, coordinate manufacturing work, manage approvals and document renewals, send relevant notices, troubleshoot problems and protect the service from misuse. Access to business records is controlled through the ERP's assigned permissions.

3. WhatsApp and email notifications

When WhatsApp notifications are enabled for a user with recorded consent, the ERP can send assignment notices, work updates, pending-work summaries and document reminders to that user's registered number. These messages can contain the user's name, a work reference, a short status or due-date summary and a dashboard link. Email is used for account communications, verification codes and reminders where configured.

The WhatsApp integration is intended for outgoing operational notifications. It does not provide a calling service or a staffed chat inbox. Message delivery and read-status information may be returned by Meta. A STOP message can be processed to disable WhatsApp notifications; other incoming messages are not used as an ERP support conversation.

To stop WhatsApp notifications, contact your ERP administrator or email the IT team with the registered number. Disabling WhatsApp notifications does not by itself delete work records or the ERP account.

4. Service providers and sharing

The ERP uses Hostinger for backend hosting and storage, Cloudflare for website delivery and security, Brevo for transactional email, and Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform for WhatsApp notifications. Information needed to operate each service is processed by the relevant provider; for example, a messaging provider processes the destination address or number and message content. Providers may process information in locations outside India under their own service arrangements and privacy terms.

Authorised company personnel can access information according to their responsibilities. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or to investigate misuse and protect the service. ERP notifications are operational messages, not an advertising mailing list.

5. Cookies and security

The service uses session cookies and browser storage to maintain sign-in and interface preferences, and security services to help prevent automated abuse. Controls include encrypted HTTPS connections, password hashing, server-side permissions and audit logging. Administrators can apply additional controls, including two-step verification and IP restrictions. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Retention

Retention depends on the record type and the operational, security and applicable legal requirements associated with it. There is no single retention period for all ERP data. Disabling a user account does not automatically erase historical orders, approvals or audit records. Backups may retain copies after a record is removed from the active system. Contact the IT team for information about a particular record or deletion request.

7. Access, correction and data-deletion requests

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data held in the ERP, email it@eurosuitsmfg.com with the subject ERP personal data request.

  1. Provide your name and the email address or telephone number associated with the ERP or its WhatsApp notifications.
  2. State whether you want to access, correct or delete information, or stop notifications. Identify the relevant account or record where possible.
  3. The IT team may verify your identity and authority before acting. Do not send a password, OTP, access token or unnecessary identity documents.

The request will be reviewed and you will be informed of the outcome. Some business, audit or legally required records may need to be retained; any applicable limitation will be explained. Requests about data independently held by Meta or another provider may also need to be made directly to that provider. Data requests are available even if you can no longer sign in.

8. Policy updates and contact

This page may be updated when the ERP or its data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version. For privacy questions, notification preferences or data requests, contact it@eurosuitsmfg.com.
